03 — How to read this siteClaim levels

Every number carries
the level it has earned.

  • Concept

    The idea and its intent are stated. Nothing is modelled yet.

  • Designed

    Drawn or modelled: geometry, CMF or narrative exists as a design file. Not built.

  • Specified intent

    A target value is written down. Not built, not tested.

  • Measured on model

    Read from the model by a stated method. It describes that model, not a product.

  • Prototype · scope

    Something physical exists; the scope says exactly what it shows.

  • AI-generated

    An image that communicates intent. Never evidence.
