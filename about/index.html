04 — How to read this siteOne vocabulary, used everywhere

Every project names its stage.
Every image, what it is.

Project stage

  • AI visual study

    An image-led exploration made with AI tools. It shows a direction, not a product model.

    EXO · BOTA · IGNIS · Catch Rider Feline
  • Concept

    A proposition defined through specification, drawings and visuals. Not a complete, measured CAD product.

    MONOLITH RF03-U1 · ÉCLOS lipstick · AHRK · ÉCLOS Paris
  • Design study

    One question taken deep — a mechanism, an interface, a form or a colour system — in CAD or drawings. Not a complete product.

    THERMA · CLX-CT-01 · ÉCLOS Refill Ritual · Relief ring
  • CAD-developed concept

    The product modelled in CAD to the depth the page states — exterior, key interfaces and the checks listed. Not built or physically validated.

    HAXĀ · ABAN · MARCELLA · ECS · AURYN BLEND
  • Prototype

    A working prototype exists (an app, a tool or a functional model); the page says exactly what it covers. Form-only models are listed under Physical evidence.

    TINY TOGETHER · PEBBY · Printed bag series · Cross-Tool Palette
  • Production project

    Released for manufacture or sale.

    ÉCLOS · TILBED · ÉCLOS Leather
  • Research / investigation

    A research output — an investigation, a method or a set of principles — rather than a product.

    ZEWM_EXCMON · Field Notes

Image types labelled at the image only when it matters

  • 3D render

    A render of the project’s own 3D model.

  • CAD view

    A clay or technical view of the CAD model.

  • Technical diagram

    A drawing, sheet or schematic made from the model or the brief.

  • Original render

    The original render, shown beside the 2026 model.

  • Reconstruction

    Rebuilt from documents or data — named as such where it appears.

  • Source material

    A board or page from the original portfolio.

  • App screenshot

    A screen from a working prototype.

  • App screenshot · AI-generated art

    A prototype screen whose illustrations are AI-generated.

  • AI-assisted visual

    Made with AI tools over the designer’s own forms or renders.

  • AI-generated concept image

    Generated from a written brief; it shows intent, never evidence.

Claim levels on figures a level, then its scope: “Designed · blockout”

  • Designed

    Drawn or modelled: the value exists in a design file. Not built.

  • Model dimension

    A dimension of the 3D model as designed. It describes the model, not a measured part.

  • Specified intent

    A target written down. Not built, not tested.

  • Measured on model

    Read from the model by a stated method. It describes the model, not a product.

  • Screened on model

    A thresholded check on the model — a screen, not a zero measurement.

  • Counted

    A count from the model or the project files.

  • Reported

    A production or sales figure reported by the designer; the records behind it are not shown on this site.

  • Supplier spec

    A value from a supplier’s published data, not measured here.

  • In prototype

    Observed in a prototype that exists — an app, a tool or a form model; the scope says what it covers. Not a test result.

  • Not claimed

    Said out loud, so nobody assumes it.

  • AI-generated

    An image that communicates intent. Never evidence.
