05 · Ergonomic carry system · Original 2020 · rebuilt 2026

ECS

Carry that moves with the rider.

My original carry system for e-bike commuters, re-engineered in 2026: a load body of about 20.5 L outer volume on side rails and a spine pivot that let it move as the rider leans. The mechanism keeps its documented principle, tension-shortening, and is measured rather than tuned to its targets.

06 — MethodHow every project here was made

Evidence
before form.

  1. 01Investigate

    Reviews, complaints, failures, constraints. Evidence before form.

  2. 02Frame

    Requirements with IDs, so every module can say why it exists.

  3. 03Model

    Parametric CAD in Rhino and SolidWorks — every key dimension a variable.

  4. 04Audit

    Walls, fits, clearances, interferences and volumes, measured on the model.

  5. 05Show

    Renders that keep the true materials, drawings that print, claims that carry their level.
