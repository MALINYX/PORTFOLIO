02 · Question

Designed from review evidence outward, not from a silhouette inward.

The category default is a soft, top-loading camera bag that treats the laptop, audio kit and cables as afterthoughts stuffed around the camera core. CLX-CT-01 inverts that: the load is organised around the spine first, with protected rapid access and independent compartments designed as primary structure rather than as pockets. The chain is traceable — review mining, complaint clusters, requirements, eight modules, form, CAD blockout — and the evidence is reported plainly.

Products surveyed (4): Peak Design Everyday V2 · WANDRD PRVKE 21L · Shimoda Action X25 · Nomatic McKinnon. No review quotes or scores are reproduced here.

1 trade-off

Weight vs protection / access — converged across all four products surveyed. The only finding strong enough to act on.

8/20 verified

Drawn from the 8 of 20 review rows that are source-verified (EV-V01–EV-V08). The other 12 are source-pending and treated as benchmark only.

03 · Mechanism

Build the blockout in ten steps.

Datums first, then volumes back to front, then cuts and placeholders last. Step through the modelling plan; the taper is drawn live, and the side hatch swings while the amber gate stays.

S10 · 4 harness mount points · rear face · placeholders

  1. 01Set flat back datum planeThe master reference: a vertical plane, 480 mm tall × 285 mm wide — the frame-sheet face and harness mount surface. It never curves.Depends · World origin
  2. 02Build outer tapered envelopeExtrude the body forward from the back plane with the V2 taper, 180 → 150 mm. The back edge stays flat and vertical; the taper happens on the front face only.Depends · Step 1 plane
  3. 03Add laptop tunnel slab inboard of the coreA thin vertical slab immediately in front of the back plane: 28 mm deep, 390–420 mm usable inside the 480 mm body. The core never touches the back directly.Depends · Back plane, step 1
  4. 04Add camera core volumeThe dominant volume, 290 H × 190 W × 130 D, biased toward the spine with its back face against the tunnel. The heaviest mass.Depends · Tunnel front face, step 3
  5. 05Cut side hatch openingCut the 230 × 120 mm hatch bounding box on the +X side — the protected rapid-access opening, placed so it never conflicts with the dock.Depends · Core, step 4
  6. 06Add retention gate placeholderA thin curved panel at the mouth of the hatch. Blockout only — it represents position and swing, not mechanism, and holds remaining gear during partial opening.Depends · Hatch cut, step 5
  7. 07Add cassette volumeThe removable cable / audio cassette, 200 × 120 × 40 mm, in the front-mid zone below the hatch reach. It must lift out without intruding on the core access path.Depends · Core front face, step 4
  8. 08Add tripod dock channelA 40 mm vertical channel on the −X exterior, with a lower anchor cup and an upper strap position — clear of the hatch on the other side. That separation is the point.Depends · Envelope, step 2
  9. 09Add spine channel and back padsThe central 36 mm recessed spine channel, flanked by symmetric ventilation pads. Crisp edges — the architectural signature line, not soft padding.Depends · Back plane, step 1
  10. 10Add placeholder harness mount pointsFour mount points on the rear of the back plane, two upper and two lower. The harness itself is not modelled — only its attachment datum.Depends · Back plane, step 1

Sizes are the blockout's starting point, not its specification. Positions are schematic; the hatch hinge side is not specified, so its swing is drawn for illustration only. Designed · modelling plan, not final
