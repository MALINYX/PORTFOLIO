04 · Key / legend

How to read this site.

Every sheet uses the same key. Claims carry a level; images carry their provenance; colours carry their source; lines carry a type and a word — never colour alone.

Key A · Claim levels8

What each chip claims.

Concept
The idea and its intent are stated. Nothing is modelled yet.
Designed
Drawn or modelled: geometry, CMF or narrative exists as a design file. Not built.
Specified intent
A target value is written down. Not built, not tested.
Prototype · scope
Something physical exists. The scope says exactly what it shows — and nothing more.
Validated · scope
Tested against a stated criterion, inside the stated scope only. No specimen on this site carries it: the furthest any gets is a prototype chip, and its scope says what was checked.
Concept visualisation · AI-generated
An image that communicates intent. Never evidence.
TBC
Provisional — to be confirmed. Drawn inside a revision cloud.
Not claimed
Explicitly not established on this site. Struck, so it cannot be misread.
ConceptDesignedSpec.Proto.Valid.

The claim bar fills one cell per level reached, concept → validated. The line under it names the evidence — a filled cell is never a percentage.

Key B · Numbers3

Measured, screened, assumed.

Measured
A value read from a model or a part by a stated method — a CAD dimension, an analytic clearance, a sampled hex. It describes that model, not a product.
Screened
A pass against a threshold: nothing was found beyond it. It is not a measured zero, and a rigid-geometry screen says nothing about behaviour in use.
Assumed
A target or input taken as given to make a decision — a load class, a hand calculation, a proposed value. Nothing on this site has tested it.

Key C · Realities2

Two realities, one set of facts.

Reality 01 · Lab · as designed

Ivory sheet. Renders, colour and cut-outs. Callouts say what the design is.

Reality 02 · Investigation · as evidenced

Dark light-table. Elevations, sections and edge drawings. Callouts say what is designed, specified, TBC or not claimed — and on a case sheet the claim ledger moves up under the hero.

Only emphasis changes. The text, every fact, the specimen record and the ledger are the same in both.

Key E · Image provenance9

Every figure says what it is.

Concept render · Blender Cycles
A render of a digital design model. Never a photograph of a product.
Blockout / grey-clay render
Form and proportion only. Tonal materials, not final CMF.
Live web model · three.js
The design model drawn by your browser. Still a model.
Spatial reconstruction render
A scene built in Blender to explain an investigation. Not a photograph of a place.
Edge-extracted drawing
Lines lifted from a model render. Not a measured drawing; not to scale unless the caption says so.
Schematic
Redrawn for the sheet from targets or a specification. Not CAD.
AI-assisted reconstruction
Drawn with AI assistance from the project's own documents. Not a measured drawing.
Concept visualisation · AI-generated
Carried by every AI-generated image on this site, every time it appears.
Photograph
Kept for a physical object and named for what it shows. A render is never captioned as one.

Where AI helped make an image, the figure says so: generated images carry the chip above; AI-assisted reconstructions say so in their provenance line, and so does this sheet's mark — a vector redraw of an AI-generated raster.

Key G · MotionAuto · on · off

Motion explains a mechanism, or it does not run.

Callouts plot themselves when a figure arrives. Instruments move only when you move them, and exploded views separate as you scroll. This sheet's mark constructs once, and replays only when you ask; pointing at it with a mouse or pen opens a lens onto the other view, with the SVG coordinate under the pointer. At most one slow ambient loop runs per screen, and it pauses off-screen. With motion off, every sheet reads complete. Auto follows your system setting.
