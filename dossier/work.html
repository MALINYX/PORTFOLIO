How to read a specimen.

Chips are coded by shape, fill and word — never by colour alone. The full legend, with the drawing inks, is on the studio sheet.

Full legend→
Concept
The idea and its intent are stated.
Designed
Drawn or modelled: geometry, CMF or narrative exists as a design file.
Specified intent
A target value is written down. Not built, not tested.
Prototype · scope
Something physical exists. The scope says exactly what it shows.
Validated · scope
Tested against a stated criterion. Physically validated: none on this site.
Concept visualisation · AI-generated
An image that communicates intent. Never evidence.
TBC
Provisional — to be confirmed. Drawn inside a revision cloud.
Not claimed
Explicitly not established on this site.
