02 · Question

Why does the case leave with the formula?

Premium lipsticks are usually thrown away whole, so the metal case and its decoration live only as long as the formula inside. ÉCLOS splits the object in two: a permanent case — cap, fluted shell, keyed locking insert, retaining base — and a replaceable cartridge holding bullet, cup, elevator, helix and collar. Instead of inventing its own helix, it builds around a supplier’s 12.7 mm mechanism and spends the design on what stays: grip, finish, and the moment the cap comes off.

4 kept · 1 replaced

Five assemblies on one insertion axis. Cap, fluted shell, locking insert and base stay; the cartridge is replaced as one unit.

Concept · architecture

The technical report lists the locking insert as “reusable or service part”.

04 · Inside

Kept · reusableReplaceable cartridgeProposed targetHiddenDatum

  1. 01Cap assemblyReusableProtects the formula and provides concealed retention.
  2. 02Refill cartridgeReplaceableBullet, cup, elevator, helix and collar, supplied as one unit.
  3. 03Fluted outer shellReusableGrip, brand surface, stiffness and premium mass.
  4. 04Keyed locking insertReusable · serviceLocates the cartridge and prevents relative rotation.
  5. 05Retaining baseReusableCloses the shell and retains the locking insert.

Proposed assembly flow

  1. Inspect finished metal shells
  2. Install cap carrier
  3. Fit locking insert into the shell
  4. Secure retaining base
  5. Insert filled cartridge to its keyed stop
  6. Verify twist torque and lift travel
  7. Fit cap · inspect the reveal line
  8. Clean, pack, lot-identify

A hex is not a CMF standard.

The hex values above are read off AI images, so they describe pictures, not metal. The report itself rules out digital values as the sole production standard for anodised aluminium: approve a physical champagne master chip with upper and lower limits, judge cap and body as a matched pair under D65 and warm retail light, and set ΔE only after supplier sampling.

06 · Decisions

Three things set aside.

A · Mechanism Concept · report decision

Own helix, from first principles

Tooling and performance risk the project does not need to own.

Selected

Supplier-integrated Ø12.7 mm mechanism

ÉCLOS engineers the shell, interfaces, finish and experience around it.

B · Grip Concept · report decision

Aggressive knurling

The report asks for grip “without sharp knurling or residue traps”.

Selected

24 shallow flutes · 0.30–0.40 mm

Micro grip, easy to wipe, and a family mark that scales.

C · Refill unit Concept · report decision

Formula bullet alone

Not chosen: refill exchange removes the complete cartridge rather than the bullet alone.

Selected

Complete cartridge

Bullet, cup, elevator, helix and collar as one keyed unit — clear alignment, positive retention.
