01 — The projectMARCELLA

My original decanter at the size I drew it, rebuilt from the original Rhino revolve: real glass walls, a standard fill, a stopper that seals, and a walnut coffret the bottle actually fits — with its gold lettering.

MARCELLA was a round decanter with gold filigree wire, a carved fan stopper and a dome-shaped walnut presentation box. The original glass was 1.25 mm thin, the stopper was glass-on-glass, and the coffret pocket had no clearance.

The rebuild keeps the drawn size and outer revolve, and makes it real: 3 mm walls (6 mm under the punt), the largest EU standard spirits fill that leaves headspace, a synthetic-cork bar-top under my carved head, and a pocket re-cut as the insertion envelope.
