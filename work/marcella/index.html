Engineering notes the longer story

MARCELLA was a round decanter with gold filigree wire, a carved fan stopper and a dome-shaped walnut presentation box. The original glass was 1.25 mm thin, the stopper was glass-on-glass, and the coffret pocket had no clearance.

The 2026 model keeps the drawn size and outer revolve, and gives it 3 mm walls (6 mm under the punt), the largest EU standard spirits fill that leaves headspace, a synthetic-cork bar-top under my carved head, and a pocket re-cut as the insertion envelope. Sealing and cork compression still need a closure supplier and a physical test.
