01 — The projectAHRK

A retrofit kit that turns one wall of a compact hotel room into a single adaptive system: a continuous anodised spine and two hard anchor points let the room shift between arrival, work, rest, dining, wellness and housekeeping reset — without renovation.

Moves and routes go to the spine; anything that holds weight goes to an anchor. Six named modes reduce honestly to two physical families — surface open and surface closed — and every module returns to one home line staff can read at a glance.

The fold-down surface carries 20–30 kg at ≈450 mm reach: about 132 Nm into the anchor as a tension/compression couple, a hand calculation, not a test. 10,000 cycles is a design target.
