01 — The project

ÉCLOS Refill Ritual

Three refill mechanisms were compared — threaded, bayonet and squeeze-release — and the bayonet was selected for its short, deliberate action and visible open/closed alignment. The track is oriented so the collar closes clockwise.

It is a digital fit study: nothing has been printed or tested, rotation checks are static samples, and the shoulder still has zero clearance — an open issue.
