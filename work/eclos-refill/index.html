01 — The projectÉCLOS Refill Ritual

A countertop skincare vessel whose collar turns 45° on two opposed lugs to release a sealed pump cartridge — taken from three mechanism studies to a checked three-part CAD fit model.

Three ways to begin again were compared — threaded, bayonet and squeeze-release — and the bayonet was selected. R01.1 corrects the track to close clockwise.

It is a digital fit study: nothing has been printed or tested, rotation checks are discrete static samples, and zero shoulder clearance is still open.
