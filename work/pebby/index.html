01 — The projectPEBBY

A peaceful single-player iPhone companion game: wander a painterly woodland with Pip, Ember or Moss, gather and craft, and restore a forgotten garden — on your own time.

The rules are the design: nothing decays while you are away, nothing punishes absence, no streaks, no combat. Barter is with local characters only; movement is 2D sprite translation with illustrated frames.

The illustrations were generated with an image tool and are labelled as such.
