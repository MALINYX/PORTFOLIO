01 — The project

TILBED

The luminaires went into production: made, delivered and sold — more than 500 pieces, as reported, across Scandinavia and especially Turkey, where they are sold under the name NOVALUX Turkey.

The turntable, the editorial images and the studio views are Cycles renders of the Rhino model of the luminaires, with a warm source inside each shade. Glazes, brass and opal glass are set from the model's layers; each table lamp's fluting takes its own body glaze, where the model gives the fluting one colour, and the celadon lamp's brass fan is set 2.6 mm proud of its model position, clear of the flutes that three of its rays cut into in the model. Each shade is carried on the lamp by its brass frame; the Rhino model leaves the frames out, so the three struts from the collar to the shade ring are added in the renders from the designer's description. They are renders, not photographs of the production pieces; the storytelling images further down come from the original presentation.
