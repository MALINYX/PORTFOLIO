01 — The projectTILBED

A lighting and ceramics collection: eight luminaire forms — floor lamp, table lamp, wall sconce — with tableware in the same geometry, modelled in Rhino and staged as product storytelling visuals.

The collection pairs glazed ceramic bodies with gold Deco linework. The storytelling images were made with Midjourney and Photoshop from the modelled forms, and are labelled as AI-assisted.
