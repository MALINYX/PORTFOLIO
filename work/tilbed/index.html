01 — The project

TILBED

The collection went into production: made, delivered and sold — more than 500 pieces across Scandinavia and especially Turkey, where it is sold under the name NOVALUX Turkey.

It pairs glazed ceramic bodies with gold Deco linework. The storytelling images on this page were made with Midjourney and Photoshop from the modelled forms and are labelled as AI-assisted; they are not photographs of the production pieces.
