01 — The projectTHERMA

A handheld hair-styling appliance built as a parametric Rhino model in staged decisions — proportion, bridge, bayonet interface, concentrator, CMF — and locked to mineral orange, warm grey and satin aluminium.

THERMA is a 40 × 36 × 155 mm handle with a detachable airflow attachment. Every key dimension is a variable, and every stage was frozen only after a comparison: five bridge variants to the B2.2 fork, three interface iterations to a 3-lug bayonet, three concentrator lengths to C2 at 82 mm.

Behind the exterior sits an independently reviewed geometry audit of whether a frozen shell can hold a real product: a lateral clamshell split, a lofted cavity and a conflict register. Flow, heat, power, noise and safety are not established — the site says so wherever a number appears.
