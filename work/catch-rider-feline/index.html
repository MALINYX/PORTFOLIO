Catch Rider Feline

A composed exterior, a vivid interior revealed in motion.

2026Concept, colour and material direction, presentation

Unofficial colourway study · not affiliated with the brand shown · AI-generated imagery

Dark green outside, sunflower inside. A dark forest exterior hides a sunflower lining. The quiet first impression releases a warm flash whenever the wearer moves. Bottle-green satin jacquard, brushed suede, a pearl leather side stripe and recycled rubber. Confident · intelligent · rebellious.

01 — The projectCatch Rider Feline

A bold everyday runner built around one idea: a composed exterior and a vivid interior revealed in motion. The Contrast Series gives one recognisable silhouette ten personalities.

Each colourway is a pair. The exterior sets a quiet first impression in suede and satin jacquard; the interior, at the collar and the footbed, holds the reveal: a sunflower, coral, lilac or acid-lime flash that only shows when the wearer moves.

Every pair carries a story, a material recipe and a mood, written as one brief and art-directed image by image. An earlier series of ten (01–10) explored the same silhouette with matching material swatches.

An unofficial study: the silhouette is a canvas for colour and material thinking. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the brand shown, and every image is AI-generated from my briefs.
