01 — The projectField Notes

Small research fragments from forensic systems, industrial design, mechanisms, materials and visual evidence. Not every investigation becomes a full case study; some remain as fragments.

Each note is one principle: a failure pattern, a material decision, a mechanism study, a colour rule, a trace of how a system behaves under pressure. Where its source has a public case, the note links to it.

They are principles, not results: none of the twelve carries test data.
