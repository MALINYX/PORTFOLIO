    01 — The projectCross-Tool Palette

    A command palette for designers who think in Rhino or SolidWorks words. Type loft, fillet, shell or hollow out: it finds the Blender equivalent and runs it, or tells you why it won't.

    Ten operations carry the vocabulary of three programs, so a search for sweep (SolidWorks) lands on the same entry as loft (Rhino). Matching works word by word: it tolerates typos like filet or bevle, and scores unrelated typing at zero.

    It refuses to guess. Below a confidence of 0.45 it offers the closest terms instead of acting; when two answers score within 0.08 of each other it asks you to be more specific; and everything it runs is a non-destructive modifier, guarded against objects that cannot take one.
