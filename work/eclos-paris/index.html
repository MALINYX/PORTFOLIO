ÉCLOS Paris

Folk-art florals and Art Deco gold, developed in Rhino. The storytelling images are AI-assisted and labelled so; the Rhino exploded views and dielines are not shown here.

Key points from the original boards

  • 01Lips: bullet lipsticks with refill sleeves, and liquid lipsticks. Face: blush and powder compacts, and a complexion stick. Eyes: mascara. Skincare: cream jars.
  • 02Brand identity: logo, tagline and visual language.
  • 03One visual language held across the four product families.
  • 04Product visuals rendered in KeyShot; seasonal storytelling images made with Midjourney and Photoshop (AI-assisted, labelled).
