01 — The projectABAN

My original flacon and coffret, rebuilt at real size from the original Rhino model: solid glass with measured walls, a ground neck for the jewelled stopper, a fill line, and a holder the bottle actually sits in.

ABAN was a bell-shaped flacon with a gold swirl emblem and an ornate stopper, presented in velvet coffrets in four editions. The original model had open glass surfaces, no neck, and a bottle passing straight through its holder.

The rebuild uses one scale factor for flacon, stopper and coffret (×2.505), set by the thinnest wall reaching 2.0 mm. At the neck the bell is too shallow for a spray crimp, so it became a splash extrait with a ground stopper — the proportions stay mine.

Decisions

  • 01One scale factor (×2.505) for flacon, stopper and coffret.
  • 02Splash extrait with a jewelled ground stopper, not a spray.
  • 03Glass solid = outer skin minus inner skin, pushed out to 2.1 mm wherever thinner.
  • 04Holder raised onto the stand and pocketed for the bottle (0.40 mm clearance).
