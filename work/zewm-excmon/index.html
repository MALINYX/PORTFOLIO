01 — The projectZEWM_EXCMON

A forensic investigation into how a cold-chain temperature exception lost its owner across fragmented institutional layers — told in six evidence frames and a reconstructed evidence chamber, REC-041.

The failure was an inherited governance condition: ownership, documentation and responsibility drifted apart until a repeated signal belonged to no one.

Colour encodes meaning, never decoration: violet for governance, red for failure, amber for warning. The chamber is a static spatial reconstruction, rendered in Blender from generator constants.
