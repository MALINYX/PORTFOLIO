01 — The projectHAXĀ

My original concept watch, rebuilt from its original Rhino model around a real automatic calibre. The case, bezel, dial art and bracelet are my original surfaces; everything inside them is new engineering, measured on the model.

HAXĀ began as a portfolio centrepiece: a sculpted Persian winged figure on a hexagon field, rendered in KeyShot. It looked like a watch, but it was a solid case with a 0.22 mm sheet for a crystal and no movement inside.

In 2026 I rebuilt it as real watchmaking. The visible surfaces stay exactly as I drew them — 2,118 case and 771 bezel vertices lie on the new surfaces — and the inside is new: a Sellita SW300-1 automatic modelled to its published envelope, a 1.20 mm sapphire under a 0.50 mm bezel lip, a crown on the stem axis, and hands restacked on the calibre's heights.

04 — Decisions & open itemsHonest about what is not done

Decisions

  • 01Keep every visible original surface; sink dial and movement 1.668 mm instead of shaving the 60 pillars.
  • 021.20 mm sapphire fitted from inside under a 0.50 mm bezel lip.
  • 03Crown moved down 0.168 mm onto the SW300-1 stem axis.
  • 04Hands regrouped into one hour and one minute hand, restacked on Sellita heights.

Still open

  • 01Phantom date: the calibre has a date, the dial has no window.
  • 02Bracelet end links, spring bars and clasp.
  • 03Water resistance 5 bar is a target, not a test.
