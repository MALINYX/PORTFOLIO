01 — The project

AURYN BLEND

The original was a surface model: the glass was a 0.3 mm skin with no real wall, so it had no measured capacity.

The 2026 model keeps the drawn form and gives it real glass: 4 mm walls, an 8 mm base that follows the punt, a ground-glass stopper shank with a 1:10 taper, and the medallion seated on its facet. The label now reads Blended Malt Whisky · Product of Spain. The images on this page are the original renders and drawings; the figures describe the re-engineered model.
