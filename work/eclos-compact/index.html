01 — The projectÉCLOS

My original octagonal compact, rebuilt at true scale. The Art Deco lid, the embossed flowers and the engraved name are mine; the refills, hinge, mirror and closure are new, and each one is measured.

The ÉCLOS duo blush compact was an octagon with a filigree lid. As modelled, the lid sank into the base, the powder was one solid block to the floor, the mirror was a 0.06 sheet and the click tab overlapped the base.

Rebuilt at 55.0 mm across the flats, it now has two tinplate refill pans on magnets, a stainless pin through three knuckles, a bonded Ø40.4 glass mirror and a magnet closure. The lid's stop was found by sweeping the hinge: first contact at 108.5°.
