01 — The projectMONOLITH RF03-U1

A permanent smoked-glass atomizer that takes sealed replacement cartridges through a keyed docking interface that does not rotate. The interface is proposed as an open standard — only the docking geometry would be shared, under one mark: U-DOCK.

The cap turns 60° and the cartridge rises 4 mm without rotating; the seal closes before the valve opens, and on the way out the valve closes before the seal releases.

Nothing here is validated: only a 3D-printed form model exists, for form and handling. The test rig that would measure travel, torque and the valve stroke is specified, not built.
