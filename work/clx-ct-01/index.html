01 — The projectCLX-CT-01

A spine-centred creator workstation designed from review evidence outward, not from a silhouette inward: complaints traced to requirements, requirements to modules, modules to a tapered 480 × 285 × 180→150 mm envelope.

The object is the load path. A flat back datum, a laptop tunnel, a camera core behind a side hatch, a retention gate and a tripod dock — each module carries the requirement IDs that justify it.

Honesty over polish: 8 of 20 review rows are source-verified, 12 are pending, and the laptop tunnel and cassette are cued, not validated.
