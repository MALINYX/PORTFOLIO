01 — The product in 10 secondsCLX-CT-01

Not a camera backpack.

What it is
A 480 × 285 mm tapered carry system for creators, built around a camera core, a side hatch, a laptop tunnel and a tripod dock.
Who it is for
Creators who carry a camera, a laptop, audio kit and a tripod, and need fast access.
The problem
Camera bags trade protection against access: protected ones are slow to open, quick-access ones spill.
What is different
The load sits against the spine, and every module traces back to a requirement drawn from customer reviews — 8 of 20 review findings traced to their source so far.

02 — The design challenge

Designed from review evidence outward, not from a silhouette inward.

The category default is a soft, top-loading camera bag that treats the laptop, audio kit and cables as afterthoughts around the camera core. One trade-off converged across all four products surveyed — weight against protection and access — and it is the only finding strong enough to act on.

04 — Design response3 decisions · reason · evidence

The decisions that shaped it.

Decision 01

A spine-centred load zone

The heavy camera core sits against a 28 mm laptop tunnel on the flat back datum; a semi-rigid frame holds the geometry.

Designed · blockout

Decision 02

One protected side hatch

A 230 × 120 mm hatch on one side reaches the body and an attached lens without a full opening; an anti-spill gate holds the rest.

Designed · blockout start dimension

Decision 03

Paths that never cross

An independent laptop tunnel, a removable cassette and a tripod dock on the side opposite the hatch, so access paths stay separate.

Specified intent · placeholder volumes

Engineering notes the longer story

The object is the load path. A flat back datum, a laptop tunnel, a camera core behind a side hatch, a retention gate and a tripod dock — each module carries the requirement IDs that justify it.

8 of 20 customer-review findings are source-verified and 12 are pending; the laptop tunnel and cassette are placeholder volumes, not yet resolved.
